Finding: aggregate throughput scales SUB-LINEARLY and saturates (+1.18 from 2->4 streams,
only +0.67 from 4->8). This is MoE-specific: each stream's top-k router selects DIFFERENT
experts per token, so concurrent streams collectively touch a much larger fraction of the
896 experts than any one stream. Batching therefore does NOT amortize the weight read the
way it would for a dense model. Aggregate ceiling on this box ~= 5.3 tok/s at 8 streams.

Base single-stream (clean, no thrash, after fixing the resource leak): 2.21 tok/s generation
(prompt eval 5.83-7.97 tok/s). Roofline: ~104B active params at IQ1_S (~1.7 eff. bits) reads
~22 GB/token; at ~200 GB/s socket BW the bandwidth ceiling is ~9 tok/s. Measured 2.2 tok/s is
~4x BELOW that ceiling => K3-on-CPU in this regime is COMPUTE-bound (i-quant dequant + MoE
gather), not bandwidth-bound. That 4x is the reclaimable headroom, and it is a kernel problem.
